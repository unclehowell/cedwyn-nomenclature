# Changelog

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added
- New entry "River Ely (Afon Llai)" (/c/river-ely/, lexicon id `river-ely`): the forms Ely and Afon Llai from Captain W. H. Smyth, *Nautical Observations on the Port and Maritime Vicinity of Cardiff* (Cardiff, 1840), p.13 and footnote, checked on the page image of the digitised copy; Alan Thorne, *Place names of Penarth* (1997), p.4, as a secondary source. Records what is not checked: a parliamentary version of the 1840 words, and the scholarly view of the derivation. Closes unclehowell/cedwyn-nomenclature#16.

### Validated
- No existing concept, variant or page was changed; 26 → 27 concepts. The new entry is found by the search box; the A–Z list on the landing page is left unchanged while the page edit freeze is on.

## [1.2.2] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- "Who uses this wording": three wordings that showed "no institutional use found yet" now list catalogue records from The National Archives' Discovery catalogue (exact wording, case and accents counted): "Llandough, Cardiff" (ADM 188/796/74967, [1917]), "Urban District of Penarth" (YHL/PO/JO/10/10/673/1063, 1920; YHL/PO/JO/10/11/355/784, 1954; MT 78/139/9, 1957) and "Parish of Llandough" (YHL/PO/PB/3/plan1888/G10, 1888, as "Parish of Llandough juxta Cardiff"). Earliest dated use updated for each.
- public/usage.json carries the same references. Same-name records for other places and personal names (Cedwin, Kedwyn, Cedwyn) were checked and not counted.
